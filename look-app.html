Planning Made Easy.

Intelligent travel itinerary planner capable of suggesting nearby attractions to help anyone explore the world — built for Windows 8 at Microsoft's Discover MY Apps hackathon.

Features

What it does

Smart attraction discovery
Automatically suggests nearby restaurants, cafes, and places of interest based on your location.
Drag-and-drop timeline
Select and arrange attractions into a day-by-day itinerary called a "timeline" with a simple drag interface.
Save & revisit
Save timelines locally on your PC and revisit them at any time.
Offline mode
View saved timelines without an internet connection — perfect for travel.
Organised by day & time
Timelines are structured by day of week and time slot for easy trip planning.
Foursquare integration
Powered by Foursquare Places API for rich, real-world venue data and discovery.
